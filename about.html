Our Story

Built on Integrity, Growing Through Delivery

Outlier Works Limited was incorporated in February 2025 under Kenya's Companies Act, 2015. In a short time, we've built a track record delivering public sector and institutional projects — including an Assistant Chief's Office funded by NG-CDF Bonchari and ECDE classrooms for Kisii County Government — while taking on residential, commercial, and industrial work.

We specialize in residential, commercial, industrial, and public sector construction, combining careful planning, compliance, and hands-on supervision to deliver work our clients can rely on.
