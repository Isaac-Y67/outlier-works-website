Projects August 2026

Completed: ECDE Classrooms for Sengera/Bosoti Ward, Kisii County

Early Childhood Development Education (ECDE) classrooms give young children a proper space to begin their education, often replacing overcrowded or informal facilities. We were glad to deliver two of these for Sengera/Bosoti Ward under Kisii County Government's Department of Education: the Nyarenda DEB ECDE Classroom and the Nyamasege D.O.K ECDE Classroom.

Both projects went from foundation trenches and brick walls through to wooden roof trusses and full finishing. At Nyarenda, the scope also included installing an elevated water storage tank to give the classroom a reliable water supply — a small addition that makes a real difference for day-to-day use.

You can see the full construction progress for both classrooms, photo by photo, on our Projects page.

Industry Insight August 2026

What NG-CDF Funding Means for Local Construction Projects

If you've seen a new office, classroom, or community facility going up in a Kenyan constituency, there's a good chance NG-CDF — the National Government Constituencies Development Fund — played a role. NG-CDF channels government funding directly to development projects within each constituency, often covering education, security, and administrative infrastructure.

For a construction company, working on an NG-CDF-funded project means meeting specific accountability and compliance requirements: funds are tied to a particular financial year, projects are typically overseen by a committee or patron (often the area Member of Parliament), and documentation matters as much as the build itself. Our Assistant Chief's Office project in Bonyando Sub-Location, funded by NG-CDF Bonchari for FY 2024–2025, is a good example of this process in practice.

For contractors, the takeaway is simple: public funding comes with public expectations. Clear communication, adherence to specification, and proper handover documentation aren't optional extras — they're the job.
